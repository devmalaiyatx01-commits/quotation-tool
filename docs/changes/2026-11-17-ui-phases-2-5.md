## Why

DESIGN.md section 2: buttons louder than the data, two palettes (the app's warm one and a cool navy/slate one that
NPD and other screens grew), a header where daily work and admin screens sit side by side, and an Add Product window
that forgets how a person likes to find products.

## Evidence

DESIGN.md sections 1-2 (screenshots and measurements of 17 Nov):
- 5 buttons on every BOQ-list row, about 108 px each;
- 9 tabs plus Preview as, email and Logout in the header;
- 181 distinct colours in `style.css`, 40 uses of the navy `#1f3864`;
- filled red Delete buttons on every floor, area and line.

## Scope

In:
- **Phase 2:** Add Product opens on the way of finding products used last (Frequently used, Configure or Search),
  remembered in this browser, with the cursor in the search box on Search. "Add and add another" adds the line, then
  opens the window again for the same area with a toast naming what was added.
- **Phase 3:** a click (or Enter) anywhere on a BOQ-list row opens it. Export stays a button. Open, Duplicate, New
  branch… and Delete are in one "⋯" menu, and Delete still asks first.
- **Phase 4:**
  - The header runs BOQs, Library, Match, Ask, then "Manage ▾" (NPD, Nomenclature, Activity, People, as the person's
    rights allow).
  - A person menu (initials and name) holds the email, Preview as…, Help and Log out.
  - "Specsheet Library" is renamed "Library".
  - On a phone the fold-out menu shows all of it flat.
- **Phase 5:**
  - Neutral colours outside `:root` replaced by the palette token for their lightness (181 → 109 distinct colours,
    365 values), and the navy accent becomes `--brown-deep`.
  - Delete buttons are red text on no fill, and filled red only inside a dialog.
  - One focus ring for every control.
  - Price and quantity digits line up.
  - Grid column heads in sentence case.
  - Reduced motion respected on buttons.
- **Found while testing, fixed:** a click that closes a menu (header or "⋯") did only that before, but with whole rows
  now opening BOQs it also opened the BOQ underneath. Such a click is now swallowed.

Out: the data, API, exports and permissions. The remaining 109 colours are real colours (status red, green, amber,
tints) or in-between greys left for a person to judge. Upper-case text typed into the markup itself (e.g. NPD group
names) is left as typed.

## Design

All in `static/app.js`, `static/style.css` and `templates/index.html`. It reuses what exists:
- `gridMenu` (phase 1's row menu) for the list menu;
- `showToast` for confirmations;
- the existing branch and export popovers, which "New branch…" opens from an invisible anchor so their code is untouched;
- the existing fold-out menu on phones.

The colour pass is a script (`scratchpad/grid/patch_colours.py`) that classifies each hex by hue, saturation and
lightness. It leaves any saturated colour alone.

It shares phase 1's branch because phase 1 is uncommitted and these phases edit the same functions and stylesheet.
Split at commit time if the owner wants them as separate commits.

## Risks

- People used to "Open" and the old tab order need a moment. The labels are plain, and Help is in the person menu.
- Colour: a grey that served as text on a coloured panel could lose contrast. Mitigation: ui-ux-reviewer contrast
  check (B9).
- Swallowing the dismissing click: a person who clicks a row while a menu is open must click twice. This is the usual
  behaviour of menus.
